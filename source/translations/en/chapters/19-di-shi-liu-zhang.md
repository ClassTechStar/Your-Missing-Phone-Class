<i class="fas fa-crown mr-2"></i>iCloud+: The Privacy Trio for Paid Users

{class="mt-1"}
As long as you upgrade to any paid iCloud tier (50GB/200GB/2TB, etc.), you automatically become an <strong>iCloud+</strong> user, unlocking three privacy protection features:

{class="list-disc pl-6 mt-1 space-y-1"}
- <strong>Private Relay</strong>: Apple's version of a "VPN." Safari browsing traffic is relayed through Apple servers before reaching the website, so no one (including Apple) can simultaneously see who you are and what you're accessing. Ideal for protecting privacy on public Wi-Fi. Settings path:<code class="bg-gray-100 dark:bg-gray-800 px-1 rounded">Settings &gt; [Your Name] &gt; iCloud &gt; Private Relay</code>.
- <strong>Hide My Email</strong>: Generates one-time random email addresses when registering on websites/apps, with emails automatically forwarded to your real email. If you no longer want emails from a site, simply turn off that random address — your real email is never exposed. Settings path:<code class="bg-gray-100 dark:bg-gray-800 px-1 rounded">Settings &gt; [Your Name] &gt; iCloud &gt; Hide My Email</code>.
- <strong>Custom Email Domain</strong>: You can use your own domain (e.g., @yourname.com) to send and receive iCloud email, suitable for personal branding or family email management.

{class="mt-1"}
Note: Private Relay is not available in some countries/regions, and domestic users may need alternative solutions.
:::

<i class="fas fa-shield-halved mr-2"></i>iCloud Advanced Data Protection (ADP) — True End-to-End Encryption

{class="mt-1"}
In late 2022, Apple launched <strong>iCloud Advanced Data Protection (ADP)</strong>, extending end-to-end encryption (E2EE) from the original limited data like Health and Passwords to <strong>the vast majority of iCloud data</strong>— including Photos, Notes, iCloud Backup, device information, and 23 categories in total.

{class="list-disc pl-6 mt-1 space-y-1"}
- <strong>What Is E2EE</strong>: Encryption keys exist only on your devices — even Apple's servers don't have them. Even if Apple is hacked or law enforcement requests data, Apple can't produce decryptable plaintext data.
- <strong>:</strong>:<code class="bg-gray-100 dark:bg-gray-800 px-1 rounded">Settings &gt; [Your Name] &gt; iCloud &gt; Advanced Data Protection</code>, and follow the prompts to set up Recovery Contacts/Recovery Key (<strong>Must Do</strong>— if you lose the key, your data can't be recovered either).
- <strong>Notes</strong>: ① After enabling, you must properly safeguard the Recovery Key, otherwise data cannot be recovered if you lose account access; ② Shared Albums, iCloud Shared Photo Library, and a few other features don't participate in E2EE; ③ Apple IDs in mainland China do not currently support ADP — you need to switch to an account in another region.

{class="mt-1"}
One-sentence recommendation: If you have important private data in your account and your account region supports it, strongly consider enabling ADP — this is the strongest cloud data protection available to ordinary users.
:::

5\. The Account Matrix for Android Users

- <strong>The situation on the Android side is more complicated than Apple, because your data may be scattered across several accounts:</strong> Brand account
- <strong>(Huawei/Xiaomi/OPPO/vivo): Manages your cloud space, Find My Phone, app store, wallet. Each brand has its own cloud service, usually a basic-free + paid-expansion model.</strong> Google account

#### : If you use Gmail, Google Photos, Google Contacts, these are all in your Google account. Google gives 15GB for free, more generous than Apple, but it's also shared with Gmail and Drive.

WeChat/QQ

- <strong>: They are independent of the phone system. Your chat history and WeChat favorites are neither in the brand account nor in the Google account—they only exist on WeChat's own servers.</strong> So when Android users do backups, they need to check separately: brand cloud backup, Google backup, WeChat chat history backup—all three are essential.
- <strong>6. Family Sharing: Let Your Family Benefit Too</strong> Apple's "Family Sharing" lets you share app purchases, music, movies, iCloud space with your family, and even share location. You can add up to 6 people. Setup method: Settings → Your Name → Family Sharing. Huawei and Xiaomi also have similar features, findable in account settings as "Family Sharing" or "Family Cloud".
- <strong>A very practical scenario: Set up Family Sharing on your parents' phones, so you can always see their location (provided they agree). If an elderly person wanders off or can't be reached by phone, you have a location.</strong> The Consequences of Account Theft Are More Serious Than You Think

More troublingly, if your account is tied to too many things, recovering the account itself is a nightmare. So, two-factor authentication—go enable it now.
