#### I. How Far Have Foldable Screens Developed?

: When closed, it's the size of a regular phone; when opened, it's nearly square like a small tablet. Representatives: Huawei Mate X series, Samsung Galaxy Z Fold series, Xiaomi MIX Fold series, vivo X Fold series. Main selling point: "one phone = phone + tablet".<strong>Vertical Folding (Clamshell-style)</strong>: When closed, it looks like a compact powder case; when opened, it's a normal-sized phone. Representatives: Huawei Pocket series, Samsung Galaxy Z Flip series, OPPO Find N Flip, Xiaomi MIX Flip. Main selling point: "compact portability + cover screen quick view".

Key advancements in 2024-2025: Hinge lifespan is generally rated at 500,000 folds (opening/closing 100 times daily lasts 13 years), UTG ultra-thin glass has replaced early plastic films, creases are visible to the naked eye but barely perceptible by touch, prices have dropped from the 10,000+ yuan range down to the 5,000-6,000 yuan range.

#### III. Buying Guide: Check These 6 Metrics

Crease Performance

- <strong>: Go to a store and unfold it yourself. The 2025 qualified standard: almost invisible head-on with screen on, visible as a shallow groove under angled light, slight depression when finger runs across but not sharp.</strong> Cover Screen Size
- <strong>: For horizontal foldables, the larger the outer screen the better (ideally 6.4"+) so it works as a normal phone when closed; for vertical foldables, larger is more practical (4"+ allows replying to messages and viewing navigation).</strong> Weight &amp; Thickness
- <strong>: Horizontal foldables at 230-260g unfolded is normal; under 230g is excellent. Closed thickness of 12-14mm; under 12mm is thin. Vertical foldables must fit comfortably in a shirt pocket when closed to qualify.</strong>: Go to a physical store and unfold it yourself. The 2026 acceptable standard is: almost invisible when screen is on, a shallow groove visible under angled light, slight depression when fingers pass over but not sharp.
- <strong>: Focus on inner screen crack repair cost. Some brands offer free inner screen replacement once in the first year; others charge 2,000-4,000 yuan out-of-pocket. This is the biggest hidden cost of owning a foldable.</strong> In-Store Experience Checklist
- <strong>Open and close it 10 times yourself—feel if the hinge damping is smooth and if there are any unusual sounds.</strong> Unfold it and look at the crease under angled light, then look at it head-on with the screen on.
- <strong>Hold it single-handed in unfolded state for 30 seconds—feel if the weight is acceptable.</strong> Try Flex Mode: fold to 90°, place on desk, try taking photos and watching videos.

Not a primary productivity tool, but great for emergencies.</strong> V. Maintenance Tips: Help Your Foldable Last Longer

##### The most delicate parts of a foldable are the inner screen and hinge. These habits can significantly extend its lifespan:

Don't Force Open/Close in Low Temperatures

##### : Below 0°C, UTG glass becomes brittle. Warm it in your pocket for a few minutes before opening/closing. This is something written in every official manual but nobody reads.

Avoid Nails/Hard Objects Touching the Inner Screen

#### V. Maintenance Tips: Help Your Foldable Last Longer

Check for Foreign Objects Before Closing

- <strong>: A single grain of sand caught inside when closing could directly crush the inner screen. Glance over it before closing.</strong> Don't Apply Regular Tempered Glass Film
- <strong>: The inner screen can only use official specialized film or UTG protective film. Regular tempered glass film affects folding and touch functionality. Recommend going to official after-sales for screen protector application.</strong> Keep Dust &amp; Liquids Away from the Hinge
- <strong>: Although most claim IPX8 water resistance, dust resistance ratings are generally low. Be careful at beaches, construction sites, etc.</strong> Apply Even Force When Opening/Closing
- <strong>: Don't violently flick it open with one hand. Opening smoothly with both hands from the center is most stable.</strong> High-Risk Scenarios for Inner Screen Damage
- <strong>Catching headphone/charging cable when closing—directly cracks the inner screen.</strong> Mixing with keys in a bag—outer screen fine, but keys press creases into the inner screen.
- <strong>Immediately opening/closing when moving from cold outdoors to warm indoors in winter—temperature difference causes glass stress changes.</strong> After a year of use, feeling "the crease got deeper" and trying to peel off the factory film—peeling the film will damage the inner screen along with it.

#### VI. Foldable "Pitfalls" & Realistic Expectations

Battery Life Is Generally Average

- <strong>: Because space must accommodate the hinge mechanism, battery capacity is compressed. At the same price point, battery life typically falls short of slab phones.</strong>: 2026 technology can't achieve "completely crease-free" — only "almost invisible from the front when screen is on." If you're a perfectionist who'll be bothered by the crease daily, don't buy.
- <strong>: After one year, the second-hand price is usually 50-60% of the new phone price—drops faster than slab phones.</strong> Expensive Repairs
- <strong>: Inner screen assembly repair costs 2,000-4,000 yuan; hinge repair costs 500-1,500 yuan. Check warranty policies clearly before buying.</strong> Inconsistent App Adaptation
- <strong>: Mainstream apps have adapted to large screens, but niche apps may just be "stretched/enlarged" with mediocre experience.</strong> A foldable is not a "better phone"—it's "a phone in a different form factor." Buy it because you need its unique form, not because it's "more advanced."
