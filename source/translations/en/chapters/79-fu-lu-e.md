### Appendix E: Thanks and Notes

Thank you for sticking with this "phone class" to the end! While writing it, we drew on a large number of manufacturers' official guides, user manuals, and publicly available tech explainers to make sure the information is accurate.

Technology advances at a dizzying pace, and the book's content is based on the mainstream technology and products at the time of writing. By the time you read it, new features and products may well have appeared. We hope the ideas and methods this book teaches will help you face plenty more digital novelties in the future with calm confidence.
