<i class="fas fa-location-crosshairs mr-2"></i>UWB (Ultra-Wideband) Technology — More Precise "Spatial Awareness" Than Bluetooth

{class="mt-1"}
In addition to Bluetooth, new phones also contain a UWB (Ultra-Wideband) chip. Its capability is <strong>precise distance measurement and spatial positioning</strong>— it can calculate how far another device is and in which direction, with centimeter-level precision. Bluetooth can only tell you "it's nearby," while UWB can tell you "it's 1.2 meters to your front-left." Common applications:

{class="list-disc pl-6 mt-1 space-y-1"}
- <strong>AirTag / Precise Finding</strong>: When an iPhone finds an AirTag, it shows directional arrow navigation like "7 meters away, go left" — this relies on UWB. Regular Bluetooth can only tell you whether it's within range.
- <strong>Digital Car Keys</strong>: iPhones and Android flagships with UWB can serve as car keys — automatically unlock when approaching the vehicle, lock when walking away, and distinguish between driver and passenger positions to prevent relay attacks.
- <strong>Indoor Navigation</strong>: "Aisle-level" navigation in malls and airports via UWB beacons, much more accurate than GPS.
- <strong>Supported Models</strong>: iPhone 11 and later all have UWB chips (U1/U2); on the Android side, some flagships from Xiaomi, Samsung, vivo, and OPPO are also equipped.
:::

::: div .info-box .rounded-lg .p-4 .mt-4
{class="font-semibold"}
<i class="fas fa-headphones mr-2"></i>LE Audio and Auracast — The New Generation Bluetooth Audio Standard

{class="mt-1"}
The classic Bluetooth audio (A2DP) used for twenty years is being replaced by <strong>LE Audio</strong>(Low Energy Audio), the new generation audio standard from Bluetooth 5.2 onwards:

{class="list-disc pl-6 mt-1 space-y-1"}
- <strong>Lower Power Consumption, Better Sound Quality</strong>: Uses the all-new LC3 codec, with better sound quality than the old SBC standard and lower power consumption — earbud battery life is noticeably extended.
- <strong>Dual-Path Audio</strong>: Left and right earbuds transmit independently — switching to single-ear mode won't cause disconnection.
- <strong>Auracast Broadcast Audio</strong>: The signature feature of LE Audio. A single phone/TV can simultaneously broadcast audio to <strong>countless pairs of</strong> earbuds — airport waiting areas, museums, gyms, and classrooms can all use a single transmitter for multiple people to listen with their own earbuds.
- <strong>Current status</strong>: Flagship earbuds and phones released in 2024-2026 generally support LE Audio, but older devices require hardware upgrades and can't be software-unlocked. When buying new earbuds, check for the LE Audio/Auracast label.
:::

- <strong>The more devices connected, the larger the surface area for privacy leaks. A few points to note:</strong> Before screen casting in public, first check whether your phone notification bar has any private messages—don't let the whole family see what they shouldn't.

<i class="fas fa-lightbulb mr-2"></i>Case 1: Battery Life Optimization in Practice

- <strong>Scenario</strong>: Xiaomi 14 user with poor battery life — out of power by midday.
