How to use this book?

What this book sets out to do

With a small book that "talks you through it and gets hands-on," it helps you move from "using" your phone to "understanding" it, and from passively receiving to actively taking command.

Reading paths

Eight parts in total:Foundations → Software → Advanced → Beyond → Practice → AI Coding → Android Development → iOS Development, covering 69 chapters. Beginners can read in order; experienced users can jump straight to the chapters they care about. v3.2 adds three new hands-on development parts (Chapters 37-69, 33 chapters in total): AI Coding, Android Development and iOS Development, taking you from digital user all the way to app developer.

Notation guide

[Button] means a concrete button on the screen;> means the order of steps;bold marks key points you must remember.

Interactive tips

Top Ctrl+K search; A+/A- adjust font size; R listen with text-to-speech; light/dark theme switching and print/export to PDF are supported.
