## V. Practical (Chapters 27-32)

{class="chapter-intro mb-4"}
Having learned the theory from the previous twenty-six chapters, it's time to put it into practice. These six chapters are all practical — from helping elderly parents set up their phones to easily choosing a new device, from building healthy digital habits to hands-on screen casting and short video shooting, to the safety emergency manual for critical moments. Each chapter is a hands-on skill you can use immediately after reading.
